      How a GPCR passes on a message

        Words to know

        Ligand
        The molecule that binds a receptor, like a key fitting a lock. In this game, the bubble that drops into each receptor is its natural ligand, and your letter tells you which receptor you are looking at.
        GPCR
        G protein-coupled receptor. A protein that crosses the cell membrane seven times and carries signals from outside the cell to inside.
        G protein
        The relay inside the cell. It is off when holding GDP and on when holding GTP. Its Gα part decides which message is sent.
        Second messenger
        Small molecules such as cAMP and Ca²⁺ that spread the signal through the cell, like a rumour passing through a crowd.
        Agonist and antagonist
        An agonist switches a receptor on. An antagonist sits in the receptor and blocks it. Salbutamol is an agonist; propranolol is an antagonist.
        Arrestin
        The off switch. After a receptor is tagged with phosphates, arrestin binds and stops it calling more G proteins.
